Bell Performance 13.21.5 — Exercise Exchange & Equipment Change Transparency

- Replace button restored on every live strength exercise card.
- Existing purpose-matched replacement modal is wired into the commercial execution screen.
- Replacement reason and scope remain selectable: today, current block, or all future workouts.
- Completed set data is preserved when an exercise is replaced mid-session.
- Remaining set load suggestions are recalculated for the replacement.
- Automatic equipment substitutions now carry location and missing-equipment metadata.
- Workout-level and exercise-level adjustment notices explain equipment-driven changes.
