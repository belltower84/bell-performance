Bell Performance 13.21.9
Superset Round Completion & Rest Timing

Build root: bell13219_work

Superset changes:
- One shared Complete Round action for A1 + B1 (or A1 + B1 + C1).
- Per-exercise Complete buttons removed from grouped exercises.
- Rest begins only after the complete round is recorded.
- Programmed supersetRest is used before individual exercise rest values.
- Final round does not start an unnecessary rest timer.
- Shared round skip and undo controls preserve correction capability.
