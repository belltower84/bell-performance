# Bell Performance 13.7.1 — Real-World Testing

## Recommended first test
1. Complete onboarding with a real profile.
2. Open Training > Exercise Library > Back Squat.
3. Review the instructional artwork and anatomy plate on both phone and desktop when available.
4. Start a workout containing Back Squat and open the exercise guide from the workout.
5. Test Favorites, Similar Lifts, and Replace Exercise.

## Feedback to collect
- Was the guide easy to find?
- Could a newer lifter understand the setup and movement?
- Was the artwork readable on the tester's phone?
- Did the muscles-worked section feel professional and useful?
- Did any text, image, button, or bottom navigation overlap?
- Were the substitutions logical?
- Did the guide return to the correct prior screen?

## Known production boundary
Back Squat is the flagship production guide. Other exercises remain at Complete, Written, or Basic Reference maturity until their production artwork is created.
