# Bell Performance 12.2.12 — Legit Powerlifting Programming

## Dedicated powerlifting pathway
- Removed Athletic Upper and Athletic Lower as primary sessions for powerlifting.
- Added four discipline-specific sessions:
  - PL-1 Squat Focus
  - PL-2 Bench Focus
  - PL-3 Deadlift Focus
  - PL-4 Secondary Squat + Bench
- Every normal four-day powerlifting week now includes competition squat, competition bench press, competition deadlift, and a second squat/bench exposure.

## Progression structure
- Four rotating prescriptions cover foundation volume, strength building, intensification, and recovery/technique work.
- Main lifts use RPE-guided top sets, percentage-informed back-offs, or controlled competition-technique work.
- Assistance targets upper back, triceps, quadriceps, posterior chain, trunk stability, and individual weak points.

## Engine policy
- Powerlifting defaults to one low-intensity aerobic recovery session, or two when six or more training days are available.
- Engine prescriptions are limited to low-impact Zone 2 work such as cycling, incline walking, rowing, or sled dragging.
- Threshold work, intervals, mixed-modal conditioning, and long runs are not default powerlifting sessions.

## Bell Core
- Added a dedicated `powerlifting` mission profile and powerlifting session templates.
- Added validation requiring squat, bench, and deadlift roles.
- Added validation preventing high-intensity endurance templates in the powerlifting pathway.

## Scope note
This improves programming legitimacy and specificity, but real athlete outcomes still require supervised beta testing and longitudinal validation.
