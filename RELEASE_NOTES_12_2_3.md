# Bell Performance 12.2.3 — Athlete Schedule Adaptation

## Athlete-centered scheduling
- First Flight now asks for the athlete's actual normal training weekdays.
- The selected weekdays are sent to Bell Core as hard scheduling constraints.
- Local weekly plans are also arranged only on selected training days.
- Training Availability can be edited later from Settings.

## Simple weekly check-in
- After a completed week, Bell prompts the athlete to plan the next week.
- Choices are intentionally limited to: keep the normal schedule, choose different days, or vacation / planned week off.
- A vacation week pauses formal strength and engine sessions while leaving mobility optional.
- Completed workout history remains unchanged when the upcoming week is rebuilt.

## Today's Mission visibility
- Mobility now appears as a first-class row inside Today's Mission.
- Optional Core also appears inside Today's Mission with its own completion state and Start action.
- Optional Core continues not to block daily mission completion.

## Better Bell Core learning input
- Workout completion no longer reports a fixed performance ratio of 1.0.
- Bell now derives a bounded performance ratio from completed sets, session RPE, and whether an engine result was recorded.
