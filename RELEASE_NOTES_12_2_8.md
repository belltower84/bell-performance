# Bell Performance 12.2.8 — Midweek Launch & Availability Enforcement

- Start Today now builds only the remaining selected weekdays in Week 1.
- No actionable Week 1 session is generated on a weekday that has already passed.
- A midweek launch creates a reduced introductory week instead of cramming a full week into the remaining days.
- Week 2 returns to the full discipline-specific exposure target.
- Friday remains a meaningful strength opportunity whenever it is selected and four strength exposures are prescribed.
- Start Next Monday continues to generate a complete Week 1 beginning the following Monday.
