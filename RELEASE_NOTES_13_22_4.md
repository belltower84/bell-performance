# Bell Performance 13.22.4 — Visible General Warm-Up Modality

## Updated
- The General Warm-Up card now names the selected modality directly on the main preparation screen.
- Examples include Easy Stationary Bike, Easy Air Bike, Easy Rower, Easy Treadmill Walk or Jog, and Brisk Walk or Easy Jog.
- The five-minute duration remains immediately visible.
- The How To guide remains available for pacing and execution details, but is no longer required to discover what activity to perform.
- Existing completed/skipped warm-up state is preserved because the general warm-up item retains the same ID.
