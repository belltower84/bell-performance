# Bell Performance 13.7.4 — Guided Tour Visibility

## Purpose
13.7.4 refines the contextual guided tour after mobile testing showed that the coaching window could feel too small or visually recede behind the highlighted interface.

## Changes
- Enlarged the guided-tour coaching panel on mobile and desktop.
- Increased title, body, button, and spacing legibility.
- Added a stronger illuminated gold border and brighter panel surface.
- Replaced target-owned overlay shadows with a dedicated fixed spotlight frame.
- Kept the selected app card fully visible inside a reliable dimmed cutout.
- Brightened the selected card without changing its layout or positioning.
- Repositions the spotlight and coaching panel during scroll, resize, and smooth scrolling.
- Improved centered introduction and completion steps with a stronger full-screen backdrop.
- Preserved Back, Next, Skip, keyboard navigation, replay behavior, and First Flight completion state.
