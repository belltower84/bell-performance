# Bell Performance 13.8.2 — Independent Daily Sessions

- Replaced the shared Strength/Engine selector with stacked independent session cards.
- Strength, Engine, Core, and Mobility each have their own Preview and Start/Resume action.
- Completion is reconciled by calendar date and broad session type, while detailed workout history remains intact.
- Completed sessions shade out and display a disabled Completed action.
- Mission Complete appears after all required sessions are finished, with Preview Tomorrow.
- Required Strength, Engine, and Core fit inside the daily check-in time budget.
- Daily Mobility is shown separately and explicitly excluded from the required training-time total.
- First Flight and Daily Check-In now support 30–120 minute choices.
- Core duration scales from the athlete's normal onboarding time availability.
