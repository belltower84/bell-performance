### Adaptive Movement Preparation
- Replaced the repeated hip/shoulder routine with session-aware prescriptions.
- Uses athlete discipline, today’s planned session, movement pattern, selected duration, and date-based rotation.
- Includes distinct preparation for squat, hinge, upper press, upper pull, easy running, speed work, hybrid sessions, bodybuilding, Olympic lifting, tactical work, and general training.
- Keeps task-specific ramp sets in the prescription instead of treating passive stretching as the entire warm-up.
- Shows **Why This Routine** and the evidence-informed programming principle used.

### Injury-aware Rehab Support
- Active movement limitations automatically replace Movement Preparation with **Injury Support**.
- A supported clinician-diagnosed condition activates a phase-based **Rehab Support** module.
- Initial supported modules:
  - Lateral ankle sprain / chronic ankle instability
  - Patellofemoral pain
  - Rotator cuff tendinopathy
  - Nonspecific low-back pain
- New or undiagnosed pain receives conservative symptom-limited support rather than a diagnosis-specific plan.
- Added affected side, phase, symptom severity, restrictions, diagnosis status, and red-flag screening fields.
- Red-flag symptoms pause app-generated exercise recommendations and direct the athlete toward appropriate evaluation.
