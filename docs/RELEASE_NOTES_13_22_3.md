# Bell Performance 13.22.3 — Evidence Warm-Ups, Plate Math & Movement Guides

## Barbell ramp protocol
- Every primary barbell lift begins with the empty bar.
- Deadlift sessions use an empty-bar RDL or elevated hinge rehearsal before plate-loaded pulls.
- Bench press uses practical 95 lb / 135 lb landmarks when they fit the programmed working load.
- Squat and deadlift use 135 lb / 225 lb landmarks for athletes and working loads where those jumps are appropriate.
- Repetitions decrease as load rises: technique volume early, low-volume primers near the working set.
- The protocol is capped at five ramp sets and never asks for a fatiguing warm-up set.

## Plate math
- Plate calculator is available from barbell ramp cards and barbell working-exercise cards.
- Supports pound and kilogram bars and standard plate sizes.
- Shows the exact plates required on each side and the nearest loadable weight when needed.

## Movement explanations
- Every integrated preparation item now includes a How To control.
- Guides include setup, step-by-step execution, breathing, intended sensation, common mistakes, regression, and progression.
- Rehab and injury-support guides retain symptom and clinician-restriction warnings.
